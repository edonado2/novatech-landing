Common questions.

Straight answers, no sales talk.

What does managed IT actually include?
24/7 remote monitoring, patching, remote help desk, backup verification, security training, and strategic planning, all under one flat monthly rate.
How much do managed IT services cost?
$129–299/user/month. Essentials $129, Professional $199, Enterprise $299. Month-to-month, 30-day notice.
Do you require long-term contracts?
No. Month-to-month only. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.
How fast do you respond when something breaks?
Critical: 15 min remote response. High: 1 hour. Standard: 4 hours. 24/7/365 on-call remote engineer.
Can you take over from our current IT provider?
Yes. We handle docs, credentials, audit, and remote migration. Most takeovers complete in two weeks with zero downtime.
Do you support both Mac and Windows environments?
Yes. Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, unified monitoring across all platforms.
What if we need on-site support?
We partner with vetted local technicians nationwide for any hands-on needs. We coordinate and manage the engagement.
