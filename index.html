( 05 — FAQ )

Questions, answered.

Straight answers before you even ask. Anything else — call us at 865-898-7648.

How much does a custom website cost?

Most custom website projects range from $5,000 to $50,000 depending on scope, integrations and content. Every project is fixed-price with a written quote before work begins — no surprise invoices, ever.

What is included in managed IT services?

24/7 network monitoring, unlimited help desk, cybersecurity and threat protection, cloud management across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, automated backups with disaster recovery, and compliance auditing — all for one flat monthly rate.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Managed IT plans are month-to-month and web projects are fixed-price with clear milestones. We keep clients with results, not lock-in clauses.

How fast do you respond when something breaks?

Critical incidents go straight to an on-call engineer, 24/7, 365 days a year — never a ticket queue. Guaranteed response times are written into every service agreement.

Can you take over from our current IT provider or web agency?

Yes — this is one of our most common requests. We handle documentation, credential transfer, infrastructure audit and migration planning. Most takeovers complete within two weeks with zero downtime.

Do you work with businesses outside Texas?

Yes. We are centrally located and support clients across the United States. Monitoring, support and most project work are delivered remotely, with on-site visits available for Texas clients.
