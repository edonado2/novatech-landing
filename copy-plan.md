# Novatech MSP Website - Copy Plan

## Voice Card
- **Tone**: Professional, direct, trustworthy. Like a senior engineer explaining to a business owner.
- **Vocabulary**: Concrete IT terms (monitoring, patching, help desk, cloud, vCIO). No buzzwords.
- **Sentence style**: Short to medium. One fact per sentence. No marketing fluff.
- **Pronouns**: "We" for Novatech, "you" for the client. No "our team" or "your business" as filler.
- **Forbidden**: "seamless", "holistic", "cutting-edge", "best-in-class", "partner", "empower", "transform", "journey", "unlock", "leverage", "synergy", "robust", "scalable".

## Fact Sheet
- **Business name**: Novatech
- **Category**: Managed IT services (MSP)
- **Buyer**: Small businesses in Texas (10-200 employees)
- **Markets**: Austin, Dallas, Houston, San Antonio
- **Core offer**: Flat-rate managed IT per user, month-to-month
- **Plans**: Essentials $129/user/mo, Professional $199/user/mo, Enterprise $299/user/mo
- **Response times**: Critical 15 min, High 1 hour, Standard 4 hours
- **Includes**: 24/7 monitoring, patching, help desk, backup verification, security training, cloud management, vCIO strategy
- **Onboarding**: 2 weeks, zero downtime standard
- **Contract**: Month-to-month, 30-day notice to cancel
- **Main action**: Book free IT audit
