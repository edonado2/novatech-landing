Nashville-specific questions

Straight answers for Music City businesses.

Do you have local technicians in Nashville?
Yes. We maintain a network of vetted field technicians across Middle Tennessee — including Franklin, Brentwood, Murfreesboro, Mount Juliet, Hendersonville, Cool Springs, and the Greater Nashville area — for on-site deployments, structured cabling, hardware swaps, and physical security assessments.
What industries do you serve in Nashville?
We focus specifically on everyday small businesses: dental practices & medical clinics, law firms, auto repair & mechanical shops, and local professional SMBs. We keep your systems fast, secure, and backed up without enterprise bloat or difficult certifications.
How fast can you respond on-site in Middle Tennessee?
Critical on-site dispatches within 4 hours across the Nashville metro. Remote response is 15 minutes for all tiers. Our local field partners cover Downtown, Cool Springs, Franklin, Brentwood, Murfreesboro, and surrounding areas.
Do you support dental practice management and legal software?
Yes. We regularly support Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, imaging sensors, Clio, MyCase, Mitchell 1, Shop-Ware, and QuickBooks. We work directly with your vendors to keep your practice and shop running without interruption.
Do you support dental practices in Nashville with HIPAA compliance?
Yes. We serve dental practices across Middle Tennessee with HIPAA Security Rule implementation: encrypted backups, BAA management for Dentrix/Eaglesoft/Open Dental, access controls, audit logs, and 15-minute help desk for chair-side emergencies. Our Professional tier includes annual HIPAA risk analysis.
Can you support law firms in Nashville with encrypted email and document management?
Yes. We support Nashville law firms with Microsoft 365 sensitivity labels for encrypted email, legal document management (NetDocuments, Clio, MyCase integration), trust accounting software support, conflict check systems, and 24/7 uptime for court filing deadlines. ABA Model Rules 1.1 and 1.6 compliance built in.
Do you support auto repair shops in Nashville with POS and Wi-Fi?
Yes. We support Nashville auto shops with managed Ubiquiti Wi-Fi across service bays, POS redundancy with 4G failover for payment processing, automated cloud backup for Shop-Ware, Tekmetric, and RO Writer, and flat-rate per-user pricing with no per-device fees.
Can you help accounting firms in Nashville with FTC Safeguards Rule compliance?
Yes. We help Nashville CPA firms meet FTC Safeguards Rule requirements: written information security program (WISP), risk assessment, access controls, encryption, MFA enforcement, secure client portals (ShareFile/SmartVault), annual penetration testing, and vendor risk management.
